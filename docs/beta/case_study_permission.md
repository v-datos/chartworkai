# Case-Study Permission

Case-study permission is separate from beta participation, payment, support, and
feedback. Store the completed permission record privately. Commit only the
de-identified permission status and a non-identifying evidence reference.

Obtain written approval before publishing any testimonial or representation of the
partner's product test. The published wording must remain consistent with what the
partner approved.

This template records publication permission; it is not a substitute for legal
review when an organization requires a release, NDA amendment, logo licence, or
other formal agreement.

## Permission choice

Select one:

- [ ] Named organization and named participant.
- [ ] Named organization without naming the participant.
- [ ] Anonymized organization and participant.
- [ ] Internal product research only; no publication.
- [ ] No case-study use.

## Conditions

- The partner has reviewed the final draft and the exact material listed above.
- Only the selected attribution level and approved publication channels may be used.
- Unapproved source code, repository details, personal data, confidential facts,
  screenshots, quotations, names, and logos must not be published.
- Permission may be withdrawn before final-draft approval. Material already lawfully
  published or redistributed may not be fully retractable.
- A materially changed draft requires new approval.
